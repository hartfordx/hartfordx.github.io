I'm a senior at the University of Massachusetts Amherst majoring in Managerial Economics with a minor in Information Technology. I am interested in transportation planning, municipal planning, IT, and consulting.

Last summer I worked as a Transportation Planning Intern at the Pioneer Valley Planning Commission where I helped install traffic counters, monitor vehicle volumes, and analyze infrastructure data using Excel and GIS tools.

I previously interned with the Town of Sebago, Maine where I worked directly with the Town Manager to improve the efficiency of the town’s transfer station by gathering feedback from residents and researching cost-saving solutions.
